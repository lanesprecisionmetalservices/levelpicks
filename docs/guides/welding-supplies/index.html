Welding SuppliesWire and Consumables Worth Stocking

Good wire and consumables make a cheap welder run better and a good welder run easier. These are the supplies worth keeping on the shelf, with what each one is and isn't rated for.

Self-shielded flux core wire for mild steel. No gas bottle needed, so it works outside in the wind and burns through light rust and mill scale.

Watch for

E71T-GS is rated for single-pass welds, so it isn't the wire for multi-pass structural work. Flux core leaves slag to chip off, and most welders need the polarity switched to DCEN. Check the pack size on the listing before you buy.

What to look for

  • Gas or gasless: self-shielded flux core runs without a gas bottle and handles wind outside. Solid MIG wire needs shielding gas but leaves no slag.
  • Classification: the letters on the spool matter. A "GS" flux core wire like E71T-GS is rated for single-pass welds, not multi-pass structural work.
  • Wire size: match the wire to your welder's drive rolls and contact tips. .030" and .035" are the common sizes for small machines.
  • Spool size: 2 lb spools fit most small flux core welders. Check that your machine takes the spool size you're buying.
  • Polarity: most welders need to be switched to DCEN for flux core. Check your welder's manual before you start.
