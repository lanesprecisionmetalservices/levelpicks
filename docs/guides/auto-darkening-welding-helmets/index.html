What to look for

  • Viewing area: bigger means less tunnel vision, especially out of position. Compare it in square inches.
  • Optical clarity rating (EN379): four numbers, 1 is best. 1/1/1/1 is the top rating.
  • Arc sensors: 4 sensors catch the arc more reliably when your hand or the part blocks one.
  • Shade range and grind mode: a 5-13 or 8-13 weld range plus a grind setting covers MIG, stick, TIG, plasma cutting and grinding.
  • Battery: a replaceable coin cell with solar assist beats a sealed battery you can't swap.
