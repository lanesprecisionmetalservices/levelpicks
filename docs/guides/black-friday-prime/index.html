1. Amazon Prime 30-day free trial

Prime members get access to Prime-only deals during Black Friday Week, plus fast free shipping on eligible items. Eligible new members can try it free for 30 days.

  • 30-day free trial for eligible new members
  • Some Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals are Prime-only

Good for: Anyone planning to shop Amazon's Black Friday Week (Nov 19–30) or Cyber Monday (Nov 30).

What to look for

  • Check that your account shows "Start your free 30-day trial". If you've had Prime in the last 12 months you may not qualify.
  • Students and young adults can check Prime for Young Adults, which has its own longer trial.
  • The trial renews into a paid membership unless you cancel. Set a reminder a few days before it ends.
  • Make a list before the sale starts so you only buy what you meant to buy.
