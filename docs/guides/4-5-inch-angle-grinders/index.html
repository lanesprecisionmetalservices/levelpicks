What to look for

  • Amps: more amps means less bogging under load. 6 to 7.5 A is fine for general work; 11 A is heavy-duty.
  • Switch type: a paddle switch stops the tool when you let go. Slide switches stay on, so be extra careful with them.
  • Weight and grip size: you'll hold it for hours, so a pound matters.
  • Spindle thread: 5/8"-11 is standard in the US and fits common wheels and discs.
  • Guard: a tool-free adjustable guard saves time when you switch between grinding and cutting.
