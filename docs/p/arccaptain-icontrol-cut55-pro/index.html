ARCCAPTAIN iControl CUT55 Pro

A dual-voltage 55-amp plasma cutter with a non-touch pilot arc. Plug it into 120V for sheet metal and brackets, or 240V when you need to get through 1/2" plate cleanly.

Check price on Amazon

Affiliate link. Amazon shows today's price, and you pay the same.

Good for

Garage fabricators and repair shops cutting brackets, patch panels and plate up to 1/2" with a clean edge.

Watch for

You need your own air compressor with clean, dry air. On 120V it drops to 35 A, so plan thick cuts on 240V. It isn't CNC compatible.

Key specs

  • 55 A on 240V, 35 A on 120V (dual voltage)
  • Clean cut: 1/4" on 120V, 1/2" on 240V
  • Max cut: 1/2" on 120V, 3/4" on 240V
  • Non-touch pilot arc, AG60 torch
  • 2T/4T trigger modes, LED display, app control
  • Air: 29–72 psi, 1/4" NPT fitting
  • Machine weight: 11.5 lb
  • 3-year warranty
